# PLA/CF Manuscript Workflow Lessons

## Reusable sequence

The productive sequence in the PLA/CF shape-memory manuscript was:

1. inspect local experimental and reference artifacts
2. reconstruct IFSS and interface calculations with auditable scripts
3. keep versioned workbooks and calculation previews
4. generate figures from calculated tables with a source manifest
5. build the manuscript from verified values
6. label computational simulation as incomplete instead of fabricating results
7. render DOCX/PDF outputs and inspect layout before release

## High-value controls

- Keep raw measurements separate from calculated and interpreted values.
- Record sign conventions and unit conversions in machine-readable output.
- Do not treat three data points or an exact three-parameter fit as strong statistical evidence.
- Do not treat interface terms as the sole driver of macroscopic shape recovery.
- Preserve placeholders when validation is missing; state what would complete them.
- Cross-check repeated values across calculation files, charts, captions, tables, and prose.
- Create source manifests for figures and generated documents.

## Efficiency lesson

Work in dependency order. Stabilizing evidence, symbols, calculations, and figures before polishing prose prevents repeated manuscript rewrites and gives the largest efficiency gain.
