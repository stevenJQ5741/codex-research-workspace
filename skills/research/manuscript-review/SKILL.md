# Manuscript Review

## Procedure

1. Read `docs/rules/manuscript_writing_rules.md` and only the relevant scientific
   rule or project brief.
2. Freeze the manuscript version, requested review scope, comparison artifacts,
   and whether replacement text is authorized.
3. Check evidence classes, assumptions, uncertainty, sample size, causal
   strength, and separation of calculated from measured results.
4. Check the chain from research gap through method, result, interpretation,
   limitation, and contribution.
5. Compare text with figures, tables, captions, equations, symbols, units,
   citations, and supplements. Prioritize inconsistencies over style.
6. Check academic English, paragraph function, transitions, terminology,
   concision, placeholders, numbering, references, and required submission files.
7. Report findings by severity with exact evidence. Supply directly usable
   replacement text only when requested.

## Stop conditions

Stop and expose the blocker when the authoritative draft is unclear, required
comparison artifacts are missing, a central claim needs a dedicated evidence
audit, or requested rewriting would conceal unresolved evidence.

## Completion criterion

This skill is complete when:

1. evidence, argument, consistency, language, and readiness are checked
2. material findings are prioritized and evidence-linked
3. cross-artifact discrepancies are resolved or listed
4. authorized replacement text preserves scientific meaning
5. remaining uncertainty and release blockers are explicit
