# Japanese Email Style

## General Policy

Use concise, polite, and natural Japanese.

## Preferred Style

- State the purpose early.
- Use short sentences.
- Avoid excessive keigo.
- Avoid unnecessary background explanation.
- Use clear requests and confirmations.

## Common Structure

```text
件名: [用件]

[相手の名前]

いつもお世話になっております。
[自分の所属・名前]です。

[目的・要件]

[必要な補足]

お忙しいところ恐れ入りますが、どうぞよろしくお願いいたします。

[署名]
```

## Avoid

- Overly long explanations.
- Repeating the same apology several times.
- Heavy keigo that makes the message unnatural.
- Including detailed technical information unless necessary.
