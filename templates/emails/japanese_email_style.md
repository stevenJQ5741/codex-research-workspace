# Japanese Email Style

## General Rules

- Use concise and polite Japanese.
- State the purpose early.
- Keep sentences short and natural.
- Avoid unnecessary technical details.
- Avoid overly heavy keigo unless the relationship or situation requires it.
- Make requests clear and easy to answer.

## Basic Structure

```text
件名: [用件が分かる短い件名]

[相手の名前・所属]

いつもお世話になっております。
[自分の所属・名前]です。

[目的を簡潔に述べる]
[必要な背景を短く述べる]
[依頼・確認事項を明確に述べる]

お忙しいところ恐れ入りますが、どうぞよろしくお願いいたします。

[署名]
```

## Tone Preferences

- Prefer `ご確認いただけますでしょうか。` for normal confirmation requests.
- Prefer `可能でしたら` when making a flexible request.
- Avoid long chains of honorific expressions.
- Avoid overexplaining research details unless the recipient needs them.
