## ILSS reheating paper

Core logic:

1. ILSS changes are observed experimentally.
2. A moving heat-source model estimates surface temperature.
3. DSC supports a high-softening or partial-melting interpretation.
4. h_c sensitivity around 4000-6000 W/(m²·K) gives only several degrees of difference.
5. Feed rates <=1000 mm/min can reach the high-softening or partial-melting range within model assumptions.
6. The mechanism should be discussed cautiously.

Avoid:

- claiming exact melting
- treating calculated temperature as measured temperature
- saying ILSS improvement is solely caused by melting

## DSC context

For Markforged-type CF-reinforced PA material:

- DSC may show Tg or broad endothermic behavior rather than a sharp melting peak.
- Broad behavior around 200-220°C should be treated as high-softening or partial-melting range unless clearer evidence is available.
- Slow cooling and increased sample mass may help reveal thermal transitions.

## IoT heat box context

Known context:

- Heating comes from indirect solar irradiation.
- Outside sensor is attached to the outer surface.
- Inside sensor is suspended inside, possibly not exactly at the center.
- Equivalent thermal conductivity depends strongly on assumed sensor position.

Use:

- apparent thermal conductivity
- equivalent thermal conductivity
- sensor-position-dependent estimate

Avoid:

- intrinsic thermal conductivity
- directly measured thermal conductivity
