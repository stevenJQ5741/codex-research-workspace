# Natural-Fiber Programs Project Brief

## Status

Working portfolio context. Validate the applicable subsection against the active
proposal or project source before use.

## Tri-country concept

- China: standardized fiber preparation, separation, cleaning, pretreatment, and
  a reusable fiber library.
- Japan: composite and structure design, interface analysis, thermal and
  mechanical evaluation, and model-based feedback.
- Malaysia: IoT monitoring, image recognition, field validation, grading, and
  feedback.

PALF is an example rather than the only target fiber. Keep standardization,
feedback, and closed-loop improvement visible; do not reduce the program to
material testing alone.

## Low-thermal-conductivity composites

Distinguish two-phase and three-phase models; discuss fiber, matrix, interphase,
voids, density, structure, and mechanical trade-offs separately.

## Short-fiber shape-change work

Relate response to orientation, length degradation, voids, interface, and network
effects. Treat response speed, recovery ratio, recovery force, and durability as
separate outcomes.

## Heat-resistant or flame-retardant printing

Distinguish heat resistance, dimensional stability, residual strength, and flame
retardancy. Do not claim flame retardancy without an appropriate standardized
test.

## IoT heat-box context

The recorded setup uses indirect solar heating, an outside sensor attached to the
outer surface, and a suspended inside sensor with uncertain position. Report
`apparent thermal conductivity`, `equivalent thermal conductivity`, or
`sensor-position-dependent estimate`; do not call it an intrinsic or directly
measured conductivity without validation.
