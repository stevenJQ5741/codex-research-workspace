# ILSS Reheating Project Brief

## Status

Working context. Reconfirm against the project sources before quantitative use.

## Evidence chain

1. ILSS changes are experimental observations.
2. A moving heat-source model estimates surface temperature.
3. DSC supplies independent but qualified thermal-transition evidence.
4. Mechanism wording must distinguish consistency from causal validation.

## Recorded model context

- spherical metal indenter radius: approximately 10 mm
- head temperature: approximately 260 °C
- specimen or bed temperature: approximately 80 °C
- feed rates examined: 100, 300, 700, 1000, and 2000 mm/min
- indentation displacement: approximately 0.15 mm
- thermal contact coefficient sensitivity: 4000, 5000, and 6000 W/(m²·K)

Within the recorded model assumptions, the coefficient range produced only
several degrees of difference and feed rates up to 1000 mm/min entered a
high-softening or partial-melting range. These are calculated, model-dependent
results, not direct temperature measurements.

## DSC working context

For the recorded CF-reinforced PA material, the matrix may show a glass
transition or broad endothermic response instead of a sharp melting peak. Slow
cooling and increased sample mass were proposed as ways to clarify transitions.
Reconfirm sample identity, cycle, mass, rate, and raw curve before reuse.

## Wording boundary

Do not claim exact or complete melting, measured surface temperature, no
coefficient effect, or a single melting-controlled cause for ILSS improvement.
